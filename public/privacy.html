Privacy Policy

Sign Renderer runs entirely in your browser. This page explains what data is (and isn't) collected.

Data we collect

None. The photos and sign files you upload are processed locally on your device using the browser's canvas and WebGL. They are never uploaded to a server, and we do not store them.

Analytics & cookies

This site may use privacy-friendly, aggregate analytics (e.g. page-view counts) to understand usage. If advertising cookies are introduced later (such as Google AdSense), a consent banner will be shown to visitors in applicable regions (EU/UK/CCA).

Affiliate links

Some links to Amazon.com are affiliate links carrying the phtbyte-20 tracking tag. Amazon may set cookies when you click them, governed by Amazon's own privacy policy. See our About page for details.

Contact

For privacy questions, reach the site owner via the support links on the About page.
