About Sign Renderer

Sign Renderer is a free, browser-based tool that previews how an advertising sign (SVG or image) will look mounted on a real building facade — with 3D extrusion, perspective, curved-surface fitting, and realistic contact shadows.

What it does

Upload a facade photo and your sign, drag four corner points to align, then tune depth, material, lighting, and curvature. Export a photorealistic mockup in PNG / JPG / WebP.

Technology

Built as a single-page app with React + Vite, 3D rendering via Three.js. No install, no account — everything runs in your browser.
